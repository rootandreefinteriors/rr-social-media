## Method
- Same scoring as last time, per platform: robust z-scores (median/MAD, clipped ±3) of log views, watch %, likes/view,
  (shares+saves)/view and comments/view.
- The rate z-scores are damped by min(1, views/150).
- Score = weighted mean, with comments counting half.
- Scored: Days 1–20 on each platform, all at least 24 h old with refreshed metrics.
- Not scored: Day 21 is **too early** on both platforms (13 h old).
- **Duplicate:** Day 19 `knocking` was posted twice on Instagram, 30 s apart and about 20 min late (01:21 and 01:22 UTC).
  One copy (`6adc12e3…`) has no metrics: they were last updated before it was sent. I kept the copy with data
  (`6ae3f0e6…`, 127 views). The double post may have hurt its Instagram reach.
- The stray empty-caption Instagram post from Oct 16 was ignored.

  - Day 20 on TikTok had 913 views at about 1.1 days, against 305 for Day 12 at 1.7 days: far ahead.

**Two exceptions show that the elements can take different forms:**
- **Day 16 "Again"** (+0.87, third overall) had no command, no prop and no "you" beyond "maybe there". Its tension came

- **Day 8 "Small"** (+0.57) had no command and almost no tension, but had the strongest R/E pairing of the early run

**Day 18 is the clearest all-four episode** and the best overall by a wide margin:

## Recommendations
**Do more of / less of**
- **More:** all four elements in every episode. Episodes with all four average +1.08, against −0.13 for those with none
  (Days 12, 18, 20).
- **More:** an immediate command to the viewer that isn't always "Shh". Days 23–25 have no command: re-cut them if
  possible, and make it standard from Day 26.
- **More:** tension that comes from form as well as creatures: repetition (Day 16), silence (Day 22), endurance (Day 21)
  or a race against something running out.
- **Less:** phone or scrolling as the "you" link. It was used in 5 of the last 6 episodes; rest it for at least a week.
- **Less:** creature-in-danger back to back (Days 18–19).
- **Less:** episodes that relate the scene to you but have no command or tension (Day 14 was −0.41 overall).

**Next episodes**
Each one carries all four elements, with a different mechanism and emotion. None uses the phone.
1. **"freezing"**
   - **C:** "Don't move."
   - **R:** a rat in the corner freezes whenever he moves. You go still when you're watched, before you decide to;
     your body still thinks it's prey.
   - **E:** unsettling.
   - **T:** a game of statues. He holds still longer each round, and the rat creeps closer.
   - *Hook:* "You went still reading this. Something in you still thinks it's being hunted."
2. **"tracing"**
   - **C:** "Hold still. Help me."
   - **R:** he traces his shadow in a moonbeam. Everyone you know has only met your outline.
   - **E:** lonely, tender.
   - **T:** a race. The moonlight slides off the shadow before he can finish. On the last stroke, the traced outline is
     in a different pose from his.
   - *Hook:* "Everyone you've met has only met your outline. You haven't met much more."
3. **"breathing"**
   - **C:** "Breathe out. On the glass."
   - **R:** his breath fogs the window. You've breathed all day without deciding to.
   - **E:** calming at first, then uneasy.
   - **T:** he holds his breath while the fog patch shrinks.
   - *Hook:* "You're breathing by hand now. You won't notice when you stop."
4. **"dripping"**
   - **C:** "Count with me."
   - **R:** a ceiling leak drips into a cup. You only know you're still here because something near you keeps changing.
   - **E:** unsettling.
   - **T:** the cup fills toward the brim and spills on the last line. Then he carries the cup away.
   - *Hook:* "If nothing near you changed, you'd have no proof you were still here."
5. **"waking"**
   - **C:** "Close your eyes. Now open them."
   - **R:** dawn light slowly fills the room. Every morning you have to be reminded who you are, and you accept it
     without checking.
   - **E:** tender, then quietly eerie.
   - **T:** he tries to catch the exact moment the room turns from dark to light; it keeps slipping past him.
   - *Hook:* "Every morning something tells you who you are. You've never once asked who."

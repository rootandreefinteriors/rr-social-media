## Method
- Same scoring as last time, per platform: robust z-scores (median/MAD, clipped ±3) of log views, watch %, likes/view,
  (shares+saves)/view and comments/view.
- The rate z-scores are damped by min(1, views/150).
- Score = weighted mean, with comments counting half.
- Scored: Days 1–20 on each platform, all at least 24 h old with refreshed metrics.
- Not scored: Day 21 is **too early** on both platforms (13 h old).
- **Duplicate:** Day 19 `knocking` was posted twice on Instagram, 30 s apart and about 20 min late (01:21 and 01:22 UTC).
  One copy (`6adc12e3…`) has no metrics: they were last updated before it was sent. I kept the copy with data
  (`6ae3f0e6…`, 127 views). The double post may have hurt its Instagram reach.
- The stray empty-caption Instagram post from Oct 16 was ignored.
- Days 19–20 are under 3 days old, so their view z-scores are provisional. Compared with earlier posts at the same age:
  - Day 20 on TikTok had 913 views at about 1.1 days, against 305 for Day 12 at 1.7 days: far ahead.
  - Day 20 on Instagram had 123, against 178 for Day 14 at 1.8 days: behind.
  - Day 19 on TikTok had 936 at about 2.1 days, against 253 for Day 13 at 2.8 days: far ahead.
  - Day 19 on Instagram had 127, against 725 for Day 13: far behind.
- **Data quirks:** TikTok reports reach = 0 for Days 20–21, so reach isn't used. Buffer has also revised TikTok's average
  watch upward for old posts (Day 1 went from 3.2 s to 5.6 s), so part of the TikTok watch gain is a change in how it's

- **More: body visuals.** On Instagram, +1.13 vs +0.10 (Days 18, 8, 13); this is the second review in a row. Day 21's

- **More: an about-him beat right after the "you" line** (Day 18 "Eighteen days…", Day 13 "which of us tilted first").
  These endings are positive on both platforms (TikTok +0.56, Instagram +0.66).
- **Less, for Instagram: Nigel claiming to sense the viewer** ("I felt you", "it was you"). Days 20 and 14 have 0 and 1
  likes. TikTok loves these, so keep them, but no more than about one a week. Low confidence.
- **Less: plot-tease endings** (already retired; TikTok −0.46). This just confirms it.

**Next episodes**
1. **"freezing"**
   - *Idea:* when you're watched you go still before you decide to; your body still thinks it's prey.
   - *Prop:* a rat in the corner that freezes every time he moves.
   - *Tension:* a game of statues. He holds still longer and longer to see what it does when it thinks nobody's
     looking, and it creeps closer each time. His lines thin as he holds (body visual).
   - *Hook:* "You went still reading this. Something in you still thinks it's being hunted."
2. **"tracing"**
   - *Idea:* everyone you know has only met your outline.
   - *Prop:* a beam of moonlight sliding across the floor, and his shadow in it.
   - *Tension:* a race. He traces his shadow on the floor before the light moves off it. On the last stroke, the
     traced outline is in a different pose from his.
   - *Hook:* "Everyone you've met has only met your outline. You haven't met much more."
3. **"dripping"**
   - *Idea:* you only know you're still here because something near you keeps changing.
   - *Prop:* a ceiling leak dripping into a cup.
   - *Tension:* the cup filling toward the brim. It spills on the last line, then he takes the cup away.
   - *Hook:* "If nothing near you changed, you'd have no proof you were still here."
4. **"breathing"** (carried over from last time; a quiet perception episode for Instagram)
   - *Idea:* you breathe all day without deciding to.
   - *Prop:* his breath fogging the cold glass.
   - *Tension:* a breath-hold while the fog patch shrinks.
   - *Hook:* "You're breathing by hand now. You won't notice when you stop."

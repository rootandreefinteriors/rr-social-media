## How this review judges an episode
- **One overall score per episode.** Each video goes to both platforms, so episodes are judged on their combined
  performance, weighted toward TikTok, where the show will be monetised:
  **overall = (2 × TikTok score + Instagram score) ÷ 3.** There are no platform-specific recommendations.
- **The per-platform scores are unchanged.** Each is the robust z-score mix from `REVIEW.md`: log views, watch %,
  likes/view, (shares+saves)/view and comments/view. The rate scores are damped below 150 views.
- **The analysis tests the creator's four required elements:**
  - **C:** an immediate command to the viewer after the open
  - **R:** the scene's action related back to the viewer's own life
  - **E:** an emotion evoked through what he says or implies about the viewer
  - **T:** tension that holds them to the end
- **Every recommendation is checked against the Bible** (the "Every episode" list, locked format, devices, the Chapter
  2 idea bank) **and the Ledger** (what has already been used).
- **What was scored:**
  - Days 1–22 on both platforms.
  - Day 23 is too early on TikTok (17 h old) and has no data yet on Instagram.
  - Days 21 and 22 are under 3 days old, so their view scores are provisional. Their rate metrics have mostly settled.
- **Data notes:**
  - Instagram has two Day 19 posts. The one without metrics was ignored.
  - Buffer keeps revising TikTok's average watch upward for old posts (Day 1 went from 3.2 s to 5.6 s to 9.5 s across
    three pulls). Watch times are best compared within a single snapshot.

## Recommendations
**Do more of**
- **All four elements in every episode.** The all-four episodes average +0.76 overall and +1.00 on TikTok, against
  −0.11 and −0.44 for none (Days 12, 18, 20, 21, 22).
- **A command said during the opening action**, worded differently each time. It is the clearest single element
  (+0.64 vs −0.01). Ideas to rotate through:
  - "Look up." · "Hold still." · "Count with me." · "Don't touch the glass."
  - "Point to it." · "Keep watching it." · "Sit up." · "Try it. Right now."

  Rest "Shh" for a while.
- **Tension built from form and objects as well as creatures:** silence that keeps getting longer, something creeping
  or filling, a test he sets you, a question he won't answer (all from the Bible's own list). Days 16, 21 and 22 show it
  works without a creature.
- **Dares and tests aimed at the viewer** ("Try to stop watching me" in Day 18; the planned D34 and D39 ideas). Day 18
  is the best overall episode by a clear margin.

**Do less of**
- **The phone or screen as the "you" link.** It was used in 5 of the last 6 new-rules episodes. Chapter 2's room,
  body and radius themes fix this; keep it that way.
- **Episodes that relate the idea to you but have no command or tension** (Day 14 −0.48, Day 3 −0.50).

**Plan changes to consider**

  as he does it". Also add a note that the command's wording, the "you" domain, the emotional colour and the tension

  Days 36–60 need the same pass, starting with D58 "Here".

**Worth questioning**
- **Dread-day runtime vs TikTok monetisation.** The Bible lets dread days run 45–55 s, and Day 16 (44.8 s) was third
  overall, so short isn't hurting reach. But if Creator Rewards still only pays on videos over a minute, every dread day
  is unpaid. Consider letting dread days run about 61 s, using a longer silence or hold rather than more words. D35
  and D47 are the next dread days.

## Bottom episodes
Days 7, 9, 10 and 11.
- They open with talking rather than something happening.
- The "you" framing is weak, or the episode is mostly about Nigel.
- Their hooks are plot teases ("I'll let you know who comes back", "I think it's deciding something").
- Day 10's draw-on device was loved creatively but didn't move the numbers.

## What the winners share (low–medium confidence, tiny sample)
- Something is happening the moment the open ends.
- A thing in the scene to watch.
- The idea turned straight onto the viewer ("you").
- An ending that leaves unease, not a plot question.

## Changes adopted (2026-10-23), applied from Day 17
- Immediate action after the open.
- A prop every episode.
- Everything framed at "you".
- Imply the silver lining; never say it.
- Hooks are viewer-centric philosophical lines, never plot teases.
- Deliberately unsettling, but not horror.
- Building tension, like a rubber band about to snap, with the mechanism varied.
- Nothing in the room is permanent.
- A chosen cover frame for every post.

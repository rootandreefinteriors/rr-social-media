refreshed metrics (Days 1–14 on each platform). Day 15: TikTok "no data yet" (refreshed < 12 h after posting),

## Recommendations
**Do more of / less of**
- **More:** open on a body action aimed at something live in the scene. It's already a rule; the TikTok data backs it
  (D12/D8/D13 +0.6 to +1.4 vs ~0 for talking opens).
- **More:** a visual that goes into his body: eye close-ups, erase/redraw. D8 and D13 are the only double over-
  performers. D21's extreme close-up of his eyes fits this; consider one roughly every week.
- **More:** let the last line land with something small and eerie happening on camera (the bird tilting first, the
  hum), not only as a spoken statement.
- **More, for Instagram:** quiet perception ideas about your senses (seeing, naming, the size of your eyes). These
  get IG's longest watch (24–27%).
- **Less:** episodes about him performing or about the show watching itself (D14 on IG). Low confidence.
- **Less:** opens where he just talks (D5, D7, D9, D10 sit at the bottom on TikTok).

**Next episodes (Chapter 2 candidates)**
1. **"breathing"** — *Idea:* you've breathed all day without deciding to; notice it and you have to do it by hand.
   *Prop:* his breath fogging the cold glass. *Tension:* he holds his breath while the fog patch shrinks; a long hold
   in silence. *Hook:* "You're breathing by hand now. You won't notice when you stop."
2. **"settling"** — *Idea:* where you live and who you love is mostly where the wind stopped. *Prop:* a dry leaf on
   the ledge. *Tension:* each gust drags it nearer the edge; it goes over on the last line. *Hook:* "Everything you
   call yours is just where the wind let go of you."
3. **"expecting"** — *Idea:* the things that live near you know your habits better than you do. *Prop:* a mouse that
   appears in the corner at the same time every night. *Tension:* he counts down to the second it should appear; it
   does, and then it waits, watching him. *Hook:* "Something near you already knows when you'll pick up your phone
   tonight."
4. **"burning"** — *Idea:* you've never watched yourself get older, only found out. *Prop:* a candle burning down.
   *Tension:* the flame gutters lower while he talks; will it last to the end? *Hook:* "You've never once watched
   yourself change. You just keep finding out."

**Plan changes to consider**
- **Alternate tension types.** Days 19–23 have five "strong tension" episodes in a row, three of them creature-in-
  danger or endurance. Mix in a quiet perception episode every 3–4 days: it's what Instagram rewards, and it keeps
  the strong ones strong.
- **Make the body-visual device recurring** (eye close-up, erase/redraw), paired with an action open and a prop.
  D10's draw-on alone did not move numbers; D13's erase did with the other traits around it.
- **Covers:** keep choosing them, but prefer frames with his face and eyes big, rather than the empty room (D14's IG
  stall). Re-check once Days 15–21 are scored.
- **Judge TikTok late.** D8 and D12 gained ~140 views each after day 5. Don't drop an idea for a slow first 3 days.

**Worth questioning**
- **How long the cold open takes on TikTok.** Average TikTok watch is 2.8–7.6 s on every episode (≈5–12%), so most
  viewers leave during or just after "Hello? Is anyone out there?". The best TikTok watch times (D8 7.6 s, D11 6.1 s,
  D12 6.0 s) are the ones where an action follows the open almost at once. The open's words are locked; a faster
  delivery (knock and line within ~2 s) may be worth testing.

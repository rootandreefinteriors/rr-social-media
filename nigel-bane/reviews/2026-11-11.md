   else" move), the only new episode whose R lands on other people; it has the top shares of the batch. One episode:
   a lead, not a finding.

## Recommendations
**Do more of**
- **A visible yes/no at the heart of the tension** — something on screen that will or won't happen, held, then
  answered (Day 29 +1.50, Day 27 +0.44 vs Days 28/30 +0.18).
- **Arc episodes that raise the stakes each day** — the crack arc was the best run since the spider; the dip came when
  the stakes dropped (Day 28).
- **The held breath:** a beat of real silence with the outcome hanging (Day 29's tape corner held the best TikTok
  watch since Day 18).

**Do less of**
- **Tension with no moment of answer** (a slow fade, a search that just stops): Days 28 and 30.
- **Absolute closers** ("You never…", "anything"): Day 26 fails the truth test.

# NIGEL BANE — Series Bible (Arc 1: "The Room", Days 1–360)

Rewritten 2026-10-23, idea-first, after the user rejected a plot-heavy draft. Where this and older notes disagree, this
wins; CLAUDE.md §1 keeps the user's notes verbatim.

## What the show is
A sympathetic stick figure in a white room knocks on the glass between him and us — our screen — and asks "Hello? Is
anyone out there?" Then, for a minute, he thinks out loud **about you**. *Waking Life* drift (consciousness, time,
perception, plain-spoken 3am philosophy) + a hint of *I'm Poppy* deadpan (flat, sincere, slightly wrong, internet-aware).

**The monologue is the show.** Each episode leaves the viewer with one true, unsettling thought about their own life —
something they'll still be turning over after the video ends. The room, its props and its strange little events exist
only to deliver those thoughts. Plot stays thin, slow and in the background. "Faster" means new ideas every day and short
device-arcs — never more plot.

One episode = one day; a tally mark on the wall for each. Day 360: he draws a door and walks out. Arc 2 ("Outside").

## The hook test (every episode's last line)
It must be **about the viewer**, make them **think about their own life**, and leave them **a little unsettled**.
- Yes: "Everything that lands in a web thinks it found something." · "Things that love the light don't know when to stop
  knocking." · "Your brain's been cutting the dark out of your life since you were born. You never asked what else it
  cuts." · "Everyone you've ever met has seen a face you never will. Yours, when you're not watching it." · "Twenty-five
  nights you've come back. I've never once checked if it was the same you."
- No: plot teases ("Let's see if the bird comes back"), puzzles ("I knocked three times. It knocked four."), morals ("It's
  sad, but also kind of hopeful"), lines that only make sense if you've seen earlier episodes.

## Locked format
- **Cold open, every episode, unchanged:** he knocks on the camera glass (his own attached arm, fist toward the lens):
  "Hello? Is anyone out there?" No title card; hard cut at the end. **The opener is the only line that ever repeats.**
- **Look:** flat black-and-white hand-drawn world, line boil, paper grain. Nigel = single-line stick figure, round head,
  three hair strands, two LARGE EQUAL galaxy eyes (the only HD/colour thing on him) with a four-pane window catch-light.
  Sympathetic, never creepy.
- **Window:** upper-right, in colour; its sky mirrors his mood and shows weather and night. No lighting tricks on the room.
- **Presence:** Nigel is the only character in the room, all 360 days — no people, no doubles. Plausible small creatures
  (spider, fly, moth, mouse) can be props. Outside the window: birds, weather, later a far-window figure.
- **Voice** ElevenLabs "Roboto", eleven_v3, ~100–130 words. **Length** 60–70 s (dread days may run ~45–55 s).
- **Captions:** tally + one lowercase *-ing* word. **Cover:** a chosen frame — distinctive pose, no caption on screen.

## Every episode (priority order)
1. **One idea about you,** said a few ways. Never a list of separate thoughts.
2. **Action the instant the open ends** — he's already doing something physical. No topic announcements, no recaps.
3. **A prop** (creature, object, weather, light, sound) that he notices and handles — the doorway to the idea.
4. **Building tension, held almost the whole episode** — a rubber band stretching, a balloon still being pumped. Vary
   it: a stare held too long; silence that keeps getting longer; a sound creeping up; something filling, piling,
   stretching thinner, getting closer; a test he sets the viewer; a question he keeps not answering. Pay it off — or snap
   it — on camera near the end. Never "small thing in danger" every time.
5. **Unsettle on purpose,** a notch more each week: a flat, disturbing truth about the viewer; the room or sound behaving
   wrong (he always notices); him seeming to sense you. Never horror, gore, jump scares or menace.
6. **Imply the lift** with an image or gesture (sun on the snow, a hand on the glass). Never state a moral.
7. **Reads cold.** A first-time viewer must follow it. No effects that need explaining; callbacks are a bonus layer only.
8. **Few shots, slow moves:** ~4–6 setups, long holds, real silences — but never a dead stretch.
9. **Foreshadow → pay off on camera** within the episode.
10. **Nigel notices.** Silent anomalies stay parked until there's a real following.
11. **A signature visual every few episodes** (a web spun on camera, threads out of his body, an extreme eye close-up, a
    long walk into the white…) — polished, or left out.

## Devices (all in service of ideas)
- **Props as metaphors.** The spider's web = what you spin and wait in. The moth = you and the bright screen. Snow you
  never see land = the edits in your perception. Pick the prop *after* the idea.
- **Nothing is permanent.** Every prop — creature or object — arrives, stays a few episodes at most, and **leaves on
  camera**, the way the bird did (D15). The exit carries an idea of its own. The room otherwise stays nearly empty:
  Nigel, the window, the tally. Plan a prop's exit the day it arrives.
- **Tagged gifts.** Now and then an object appears with a paper tag, "for nigel." Not his handwriting; there's no door. A
  quiet, unsettling background question (who keeps this room?) — never the episode's subject.
- **Knocks from our side** happen rarely and only when they serve the day's idea. Never explained in Arc 1.
- **Dread days** (2 per chapter): something is badly wrong and Nigel knows it — but they are still built on an idea about
  the viewer, said plainly. Full polish, never glitches.
- **Parked until there's an audience:** silent anomalies, rerun days, caption tricks, unremarked oddities.

## Chapter 1 — The Glass (D1–25) · *Is anyone out there?* — DONE
Attention, perception, proof, presence. You only see the past (D1) · you can't prove anyone's real (D2) · same room,
different weather (D3) · memory is notes from people who used to be you (D4) · you rebuild memories (D5) · names stop you

reflex (D16) · the web you spin (D17) · you don't feel your threads until you try to leave (D18) · drawn to the brightest
thing in the room (D19) · wanting to be reached so badly you invent it (D20) · your brain edits out the dark (D21) · what's
under your noise (D22) · DREAD: everything past where you stopped walking is trust (D23) · you've never seen your own face
(D24) · nobody proves anyone's real; you keep showing up (D25).

## Chapter 2 — The Room (D26–60) · *What is "here"?*
The room you're in right now, the body you're in, the radius of your life, comfort as confinement, the people and
systems that keep you that you never see. Opens with the spider's exit (D26); then the crack (D27–30) and tagged gifts
(from D31) — each prop leaves within a few episodes. A suggested running order — reorder freely, but keep each line's idea, prop and hook intact.

- **26 · Gone** — *You never see things arrive or leave. You just notice, one morning, that they've been gone a
  while.* The spider is gone; the web is empty and torn, the bundles with it. He searches the corner (tension: where?);
  one silk thread glints up into nothing. "She got in. I don't know how. She got out. I don't know how either." When he
  touches the web it lets go and drifts down in pieces — the corner is bare. → "You never see anything leave. You just
  notice, one morning, that it's been gone for a while."
- **27 · Crack** — *You stop seeing damage after about a week.* A hairline crack appears low on our side of the glass
  (polished, phone-crack thin); it creeps while he talks. → "Whatever's been cracked in your life the longest… you
  stopped seeing it about a week in."
- **28 · Used to it** — *You can get used to anything — that's how people stay in rooms they should leave.* The crack is
  bigger; midway he stops looking at it, and we notice he stopped. → "You can get used to anything. That's how you end
  up staying."
- **29 · Taped** — *You've been fixed by people you'll never thank.* Tape over the crack, on our side. → "Somebody
  fixed something for you today. You'll never find out what."
- **30 · Healed** — *Healing is mostly forgetting exactly where it hurt.* Clean glass; his hand searches for where it
  was. "Glass doesn't heal." → "You heal by forgetting where it was. Then you can't find it again."
- **31 · A tag** — *Almost nothing in your room did you choose.* A paper tag on the floor: "for nigel." → "Look
  around. Count what you actually chose."
- **32 · The chair** — *Everything made for you is shaping you* (the chair decides how you sit; the phone how you hold
  your head). A chair with the matching tag. → "Everything made for you is also making you."
- **33 · Won't sit** — *Comfort is a kind of holding.* He circles the chair and won't sit (tension: will he?). →
  "Every comfortable thing is holding you a little."
- **34 · Sits** — *Habits are furniture — you become the shape of what you sit in.* → "After a while it isn't you
  sitting in the chair. It's the chair sitting in you."
- **35 · DREAD: Upstairs** — *You've explained away every sound in your home and never checked one.* Footsteps cross
  the ceiling. There is no upstairs. He doesn't move. → "You've decided what every sound in your house is. You've never
  once gone to look."
- **36 · Empty room** — *Your room spends more time without you than with you.* He walks out of frame; we hold on the
  empty room; he talks from off-screen. → "Nobody has ever seen what your room does when you're not in it."
- **37 · Ceiling** — *There's a whole surface over you every night you never look at.* He lies down, looks up. →
  "Something has been above you every night of your life. You've never once looked up at it."
- **38 · Dust** — *Some of the dust in your room used to be you.* Dust turning in a sunbeam; he holds out a hand. →
  "You've been slowly turning into your room."
- **39 · Two rooms** — *You're in two rooms right now: that one, and the one in your hand.* He asks you to look up for
  three seconds — and waits (test). → "That room's been waiting behind you the whole time."
- **40 · Light left on** — *You leave a light on so the room isn't empty when you come back — as if you're two people.*
  A tagged lamp, which burns out by the end (one-episode prop). → "You keep a light on for yourself. Like someone's
  coming home who isn't you."
- **41 · Corner** — *Every room has a corner nobody looks at.* The bare corner where the web was. → "There's a corner in your room
  you haven't looked at in years. It's looked at you every day."
- **42 · Nine places** — *Your whole life happens in about nine places.* He counts steps from the window to the tally:
  twelve. → "Your whole life fits in about nine places. You've never counted them."
- **43 · Eleven steps** — *Lives shrink so slowly you never notice — the friend you stopped calling, the street you
  stopped taking.* Eleven steps. → "Nobody decides to make their life smaller. It just loses a step at a time."
- **44 · Nine steps** — *Measuring a life makes it smaller.* Nine; the window is nearly over the tally; he stops
  counting. → "The more carefully you count your life, the less of it there is."
- **45 · Home smell** — *Your home has a smell you'll only notice if you're gone long enough to forget it.* A tagged
  cup, steaming, drunk and gone by the end. → "You only notice home once it's stopped being yours."
- **46 · Doorways** — *Walking through a doorway makes you forget why you came in — your brain files the old room away.*
  There's no door; he keeps walking the same path. → "Every room you've ever left still has a little of you in it."
- **47 · DREAD: Can't look** — *You've never had a single minute where nobody could see you.* The whole monologue
  facing the wall; at the end a knock from our side. He flinches, doesn't turn. → "Don't."
- **48 · Echo** — *Something in you has heard every thought you never said.* The room starts echoing his last words
  back, a beat late (he notices). → "Someone in there has heard every single thing you've never said."
- **49 · Wallpaper** — *You stopped seeing your view years ago.* The same cloud in the window, every day this week —
  he only now notices. → "The view stopped being a view the day you stopped looking at it."
- **50 · Breath** — *The air in your room has been in other lungs.* His breath fogs our glass (polished). → "You're
  breathing someone right now."
- **51 · Rectangle** — *You'll spend a third of your life unconscious in a rectangle, trusting the door.* He lies down
  on the floor by the chair. → "You hand yourself over every night and call it rest."
- **52 · Re-inked** — *Something keeps you running all night that never asks permission* (your heart, your liver, the
  people on night shift). The tally has been neatly re-inked overnight. → "Something kept you going all night. It
  didn't ask. It never will."
- **53 · Taken back** — *Nothing you have is yours; it's all on loan.* The chair is gone; only its tag is left on
  the floor. → "Nothing you own is yours. You're just the last one holding it."
- **54 · Body** — *You've never been anywhere without your body.* Signature: his outline glows as he traces it. →
  "You've never once been anywhere it didn't take you."
- **55 · After you** — *Someone will sleep where you sleep and never know you existed.* He scratches a tag of his own,
  "for whoever," and leaves it on the floor. → "Someone will sleep where you sleep and never once think of you."
- **56 · Thank you** — *You've been thanked for things you don't remember doing.* His tag is gone; in its place: "for
  nigel. thank you." → "Somebody still thinks about something you said once. You don't even remember saying it."
- **57 · 3am** — *Some rooms you only live in at 3am.* Night; the chair's shadow has moved (he sees it). → "There's a
  version of your room you only meet at three in the morning. It knows you better."
- **58 · Here** — *Wherever you are, you call it "here." You've never once been anywhere else.* → "Everything that
  isn't here is just a rumour you believe."
- **59 · Keeper** — *Someone made almost everything you touched today. You'll never meet them.* A new tag arrives on a
  small blanket (one episode). → "Every object you own is a note from a stranger."
- **60 · Stopped looking (CHAPTER END)** — *Home is wherever you stopped looking for somewhere else.* Everything that
  arrived has gone again; the room is as empty as the first day. He looks around it as if for the first time. → "Here is just
  wherever you stopped looking for somewhere else."

## Chapters 3–12 — territories and seed ideas
Each gets a full idea bank like Chapter 2 about two weeks before we reach it. Seeds below are starting points, not a
schedule. Keep the eye-colour milestones and Day 360.

**Ch 3 — Days (D61–90) · What is a life made of?** Routine, repetition, time, habit, Sisyphus. Props: a morning ritual,
a tagged cup (rain fills it), the tally itself. Seeds: *your life is mostly Tuesdays* · *you'll forget almost every day
you've lived, including this one* · *there was a last time your parent carried you, and nobody knew* · *time speeds up
because you stopped having firsts* · *your habits are a person who does your life for you* · *the version of you on
autopilot has lived more of your life than you have* · *you've already had your last day of something you love* ·
*Sunday night is grief for a day that isn't over*. Dread: the window is bricked over ("They bricked it up. Or I did. In
my sleep.") · he hears his own voice whispering the opener when he isn't talking. Landing D90: he chooses the repeating
day anyway; first gold eyes (flicker ~D84, full ~D88).

**Ch 4 — The Other Window (D91–120) · Are there other minds?** Sonder, empathy, the people you'll never know. Prop: a far
window across the way; a light; a figure (stays). Seeds: *everyone you passed today has a head as loud as yours* · *you're
an extra in thousands of strangers' days* · *someone still remembers the time you were unkind and you don't* · *empathy is
a very good guess* · *you've been someone's "that guy"* · *the stranger who saw you cry on a train* · *waving at someone
who might not wave back* · *you only ever meet the outside of people*. Dread: he watches the far window for nine hours; it
never moves · marks multiply on the wall behind him mid-sentence. Landing D120: they press their hands to their glass at
the same moment.

**Ch 5 — Witness (D121–150) · What does it mean to be seen?** Being watched, performing, envy, comparison, attention as
love. Props: a tagged plant; the figure's lamp; their tally (higher than his); they hold up a drawing of him. Seeds:
*you're different when someone's watching, and someone is almost always watching* · *envy is grief for a life you never
had* · *you're being compared right now by someone you'll never meet* · *being looked at isn't being seen* · *the best
version of you exists only in other people's photos*. Dread: the figure mirrors his stance all episode — then, when he
moves, it doesn't · every time he blinks the window is somewhere else. Landing D150: silver eyes; not alone. (Green
"envy" eyes ~D128.)

**Ch 6 — Dark Window (D151–180) · What's left when someone goes?** Absence, waiting, the last time, grief. Prop: the far
window goes dark; the figure's drawings fade in the sun one by one; a shadow returns. Seeds: *you don't know which time
was the last time* · *grief is love with nowhere to go* · *you keep someone's number long after* · *waiting changes who
comes back* · *absence has a shape exactly like the person*. Dread: a vigil at the dark window; two galaxy-coloured dots
blink inside · he crosses out marks one by one: "I'm checking which ones were really me." Landing D180: the light comes
back on; the plant flowers.

**Ch 7 — The Line (D181–210) · Are you still you?** Change, identity, Ship of Theseus. Device: the line boil itself — he
catches himself being redrawn; a hair strand erased and redrawn differently. Seeds: *most of your body has replaced itself
since you were a child* · *your memories are copies of copies* · *the person who started this year is gone* · *you'd be a
stranger to yourself at eight* · *you keep the name; nothing else stays*. Dread: the room and his body erase from the edges
in ("It's okay. It's just the edges.") · he walks up the wall and across the ceiling ("The floor's more of a suggestion
today."). Landing D210: same Nigel, new lines.

**Ch 8 — Erasure (D211–240) · What happens when you're gone?** Mortality, being forgotten, what outlasts you. Props: the
plant wilts and dies (a tag on the pot: "sorry"), a sprout; a feather on the sill, a bird returns. Seeds: *in three
generations nobody will know your name* · *your things will outlive you and won't know* · *someone will one day say your
name for the last time* · *you're already in someone's camera roll forever* · *the dead outnumber us and we live in their
rooms*. Dread: the plant is gone; he talks to the empty pot as if it's there · a knock, the room empty, his voice from
somewhere: "Don't answer it." Landing D240: renewal — and someone cares.

**Ch 9 — Small Things (D241–270) · What's enough?** Gratitude, smallness, absurd joy, the ordinary. Devices: he starts
answering your comments; he finally names things; two birds. Seeds: *the best day of your life was probably ordinary and
you didn't notice* · *you're the luckiest arrangement of atoms in the history of atoms* · *joy is mostly paying attention* ·
*you'll miss today*. Dread: the camera is outside, across the way; he's inside knocking silently ("I wonder what I look
like from out there.") · a tag in *his* handwriting: "for you." Landing D270: *he* knocks first, and something answers.

**Ch 10 — The Pencil (D271–300) · Are you allowed to make things?** Creation, fear, permission. Props: a pencil (untagged —
from you?), his first dot, line, erased line, kept shape. Seeds: *nobody gives you permission; they just stop stopping you*
· *every made thing was made by someone scared* · *you've been waiting to be told you're allowed* · *the first mark is the
whole fear*. Devices: he asks you to describe yourselves in the comments and draws a face from them. Dread: the pencil
stands on its point all morning · the tally rearranged into a rectangle. Landing D300: his first real drawing — a circle
(a sun).

**Ch 11 — Content (D301–330) · Can you just be happy?** Contentment and the suspicion of it. Seeds: *you brace during the
good parts* · *happiness feels like something you'll be charged for* · *peace is boring until it's gone*. Devices: trading
drawings with the far figure; gifts he leaves for the keeper. Dread: he smiles the whole episode, "I'm fine. I'm fine
today." · circles draw themselves on the wall and he's delighted. Landing D330: the first bare mention of circles.

**Ch 12 — Full Circle (D331–360) · fixation → release.** The one chapter that builds a single thread: the circle
obsession dials up a notch a day; hair-thin ring cracks return in the glass (D358); the perfect circle changes nothing and
the rings spread across the whole screen (D359). Dread: a perfect loop — he ends on the first half of the sentence he
started with ("Have I said this before?") · the whole room is round ("Finally."). Landing: **Day 360.**

## Day 360 — Full Circle
360 degrees, 360 days. He looks at the window, then (close on his eyes) at the four-pane catch-light that has been in his
eyes since Day 1. It was never about circles. It was always rectangles: the window, the glass he knocks on, the screen
you're holding. He picks up the pencil, draws a rectangle on the wall, a doorknob. A door. On the knob: a tag, "for
nigel." The knock comes from the door. He opens it. Light. He walks out. The tally reads exactly 360. Day 359 ended with
ring cracks across the whole screen, the glass about to go; today it holds. The way out was never through the glass.
**Arc 2 bridge:** pull back — his room is one window in an enormous building, a figure in every window, knocking. Below:
a small desk covered in paper tags. The keeper is never shown.

**Hidden seeds (never point at them):** the four-pane catch-light = the window = the door · circles and rectangles
everywhere since Day 1 · "the glass gets a little thinner" (D1) → the crack (D26–29) → ring cracks (D358) → the door.

## Spine
- **Tally:** one mark per day, groups of five, left wall; usually already there, drawn on camera now and then (D25 closed
  the fifth hand). Milestones: D100 · D300 · D360 exactly 360.
- **Eye colours:** start set (curious, uncanny, drifting, awe, melancholy, lonely, warm, unease, void). Unlocks: gold
  "hope" ~D84–88 · green "envy" ~D128 · silver "peace" D150 · near-white "fixation" D345+.
- **Props come and go.** The spider and her web (D17–25, with the wrapped fly and moth) leave on D26. Long-running
  objects named in the Ch3–12 seeds (the plant, the pencil, the far figure's lamp) get planned exits when each chapter is
  detailed — nothing stays for good.

## Post captions
- The tally + ONE cryptic lowercase **-ing** word, nothing else: `IIIII III humming`. The word is the ominous angle under
  the episode, never its obvious subject. Capital `I` in groups of five, space-separated.
- Day 360: no caption. Generate the tally: `python -c "n=23; print(' '.join('I'*5 for _ in range(n//5)) + (' ' + 'I'*(n%5) if n%5 else ''))"`

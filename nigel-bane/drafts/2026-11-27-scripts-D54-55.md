## D54 · `orbiting` · The warm part (sun 1/2)
- **The line:** "You never decided to follow the warm thing. You just end up wherever it is."
- **Bridge:** he keeps shuffling after the patch as it moves across the floor → you end up wherever the person who
  warms you is, and sit in the warm place they leave.
- **Scene:** afternoon, the room cold (he hugs his arms, a small shiver). A patch of sun lies on the floor; he sits in
  it. Over the episode it slides toward the left wall and shrinks; he shuffles after it three times, each time it's
  smaller. Payoff on camera: he pulls his knees up and curls small; he fits, just.
- **Featured close sound:** the soft scuff of him shuffling across the floor. One whispered stretch: "I fit. Just."
- **Truth test:** "probably".

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Come here. Sit in it with me. ...It's the only warm part of the room. It
> keeps moving. So I keep moving. ...I didn't decide to. I just end up wherever it is. You probably do this with a
> person. You end up in whatever room they're in. You sit on their side of the bed after they get up, because it's
> still warm. You never decided that either. [uneasy] ...It's getting smaller. Knees up. [whispers] I fit. Just.
> ...I didn't decide that either.

## D55 · `homing` · Where it comes back (sun 2/2, the patch leaves)
- **The line:** "I can't keep it. I just know where it comes back."
- **Bridge:** the patch leaves every afternoon and comes back every morning to the same place on the floor → you know
  the exact sound of the person who warms you coming home, before you know you're listening.
- **Scene:** late afternoon. The patch is a thin strip climbing the left wall. He stands with both palms in it,
  following it up, on tiptoe, arm at full stretch (both hands kept out to the side, below the head); it slides above
  his reach and thins out to nothing. The room goes evenly cold-grey. He sits down on the bare floor, on the spot by
  the window where it arrives each morning, and puts his hand flat there. Hope implied by the image, not said.
- **Truth test:** about the patch, literally true; "if someone comes home to you" narrows the claim.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Stand up with me. Quick. It's going. ...It climbs the wall every afternoon.
> I keep my hands in it as long as I can. ...Up. ...I can't reach. [uneasy] ...It's gone. It's cold now. Everywhere.
> ...It's alright. It comes back in every morning. Right here. I could find this spot with my eyes shut. If someone
> comes home to you, you know the sound of their key in the door. You hear it before you know you're listening.
> [quietly] I can't keep it. ...I just know where it comes back.

(~95 words)

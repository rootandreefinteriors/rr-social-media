## D49 · `tracing` · Hand (body 1/4)
- **The line:** "You've never once been anywhere it wasn't." (your body)
- **Bridge:** the outline is the only shape he's ever been → it has been everywhere you've ever been.
- **Scene:** day. He presses his palm flat to the glass; a thin line slowly draws round it on our side, finger by finger.
  He holds still for it. It closes on "There." He lifts his hand: the outline stays on the glass.
- **Unsettling moment:** the line drawing itself on our side of the glass, and "Don't let me move."
- **Truth test:** literally true.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Hold your hand up. Look at the back of it. ...I'm putting mine on the glass.
> Flat. Something's drawing round it. On your side. [quietly] Don't let me move. ...It goes round every finger.
> Slowly. Like it wants to get me right. I've never seen my own outline before. That's the only shape I've ever been.
> Every room I've stood in, it was standing in too. You've got one. You're holding it up. It was there the day you
> were born. It was there the worst night you ever had. You've never once been anywhere it wasn't. [whispers]
> Nearly... nearly... ...There. ...You can put yours down now. It's still yours.

## D50 · `shedding` · Doesn't fit (body 2/4)
- **The line:** "The hand you just put down isn't quite the one you had last month. There was no day it happened."
- **Bridge:** his hand no longer fits its own outline and he never felt it change → your skin is replacing itself
  while you watch this.
- **Scene:** morning. The outline from yesterday is on the glass, shown and named. He fits his palm back in: the thumb
  sits outside the line. Tries again, slower: still out.
- **Truth test:** skin renews over weeks; "isn't quite" and "last month" keep it true.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Put your hand down flat. On your leg. The table. Anything. Leave it there.
> ...Yesterday something drew round my hand. Right here. On your side of the glass. I'm putting it back. ...It doesn't
> fit. The thumb's out. [uneasy] Again. ...It still doesn't fit. I didn't feel anything change. I've been in it the
> whole time. Your skin does this. Bit by bit, while you sleep, while you're watching this. The hand you just put
> down isn't quite the one you had last month. There was no day it happened. ...Lift it up. Look at it. Is that the
> same hand? ...How would you know?

## D51 · `shaping` · The right shape (body 3/4)
- **The line:** "You've been trying to get back inside a picture of yourself. It was only ever where you stood once."
- **Bridge:** he keeps trying to fit a tracing of himself → you keep trying to fit an old photo of yourself.
- **Scene:** afternoon. He shifts his hand in the outline again and again, a fraction each time, tongue-out careful;
  then stops and leaves it half in, half out, and leans his cheek on the glass beside it.
- **Truth test:** "probably" on the photo; the rest is his own claim.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Don't fix anything. Stay how you're sitting. ...I'm nearly in it. A little
> left. [softly] There. ...No. My fingers are too long for it now. Or it's too short. Something drew round me once,
> and now I keep trying to fit the drawing. It isn't even me. It's just where I was. There's probably a photo of you
> like that. A summer, years ago. Someone said you looked good. You've been trying to get back inside it ever since.
> ...I'm going to stop now. Half in. Half out. ...It was only ever where I stood once. ...It doesn't feel so bad,
> leaving it like this.

(~105 words)

## D52 · `slowing` · Heartbeat (body 4/4, the outline leaves)
- **The line:** "Your heart has never once asked you for anything."
- **Bridge:** he hears his own heart through the outline, keeping on without being asked → yours, under your hand.
- **Scene:** night. He lays his ear on the glass where the outline is; we hear a heartbeat (engine SFX `heart`), fast,
  then slowing; long gaps; he stops breathing to listen. It settles into a calm beat, and the outline fades with it.
- **Personal-attention moment** (Bible rule 11 lists D52): hand on your chest, one whispered stretch.
- **Truth test:** a heart doesn't ask; true as said.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Shh. Put your hand on your chest. ...I've put my ear where my hand was. I can
> hear my heart in it. [whispers] Listen. ...It's slowing down. ...Don't stop. ...Don't stop. It's been doing that
> since before you could talk. Through every night you didn't sleep. Through the day you thought you couldn't keep
> going. It kept going anyway, in the dark, and nobody thanked it. It's doing it now, under your hand. ...It's quieter.
> It isn't stopping. It's just calm. [softly] Your heart has never once asked you for anything. ...The outline's
> going. Let it.

(~105 words)

## D53 · `overlooking` · Stuck cloud (seeing 1/3)
- **The line:** "There's probably someone who's been in the same place in your life for years. You stopped seeing them
  a long time ago."
- **Bridge:** the one cloud that never moves becomes part of the window and he stops seeing it → the person who is
  always there.
- **Scene:** day. Clouds drift across the window; one in the middle stays put. He watches it, checks it, names it.
  He turns to us for one sentence; when he turns back it's gone.
- **Truth test:** "probably"; "you'd notice the day they weren't there" is true of most people.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Watch the cloud with me. That one. In the middle. ...The others are going past
> it. It hasn't moved since this morning. I keep checking. [quietly] Still there. Everything else goes by, and it just
> stays. I stopped seeing it about lunchtime. It was just part of the window. There's probably someone in your life
> like that. Same place for years. Same chair. Same "how was your day." You stopped seeing them a long time ago. You'd
> notice the day they weren't there. ...I looked at you for one second. ...It's gone. When did you last really look at
> them?

(~110 words)

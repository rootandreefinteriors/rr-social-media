## D49 · `tracing` · Hand (body 1/4)
- **The line:** "You've never once been anywhere it wasn't." (your body)
- **Bridge:** the outline is the only shape he's ever been → it has been everywhere you've ever been.
- **Tension (love):** he holds his palm to the glass and asks you to hold yours up to it and stay until the line
  closes; he needs you there. The line visibly creeps round each finger; it closes on "There."
- **Scene:** day. Palm flat to the glass; a thin line slowly draws round it on our side, finger by finger. He holds
  still, looking at where your hand would be. It closes; he lifts his hand: the outline stays on the glass.
- **Unsettling moment:** the line drawing itself on our side of the glass; "You stayed." (he seems to know).
- **Truth test:** literally true.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Hold your hand up. Up to mine. ...There. Something's drawing round my hand.
> On your side of the glass. [quietly] Stay there till it's done. Please. ...It goes round every finger. Slowly. Like
> it wants to get me right. I've never seen my own outline before. That's the only shape I've ever been. Every room
> I've stood in, it was in there with me. Yours is right there, against mine. It was there the day you were born. It
> was there the worst night you ever had. You've never once been anywhere it wasn't. [whispers] Nearly... don't go...
> ...There. ...You stayed. You can put yours down now. It's still yours.

## D50 · `shedding` · Doesn't fit (body 2/4)
- **The line:** "The hand you just put down isn't quite the one you had last month. There was no day it happened."
- **Bridge:** his hand no longer fits its own outline and he never felt it change → your skin is replacing itself
  while you watch this.
- **Tension (fear):** his own hand is changing under him and he can't stop it. Each try fits worse (thumb out, then
  fingers out, then further); will it ever fit again? It doesn't.
- **Scene:** morning. The outline from yesterday is on the glass, shown and named ("It fit me exactly"). Three tries,
  each slower and more careful, each visibly worse; he notices and says so every time.
- **Truth test:** skin renews over weeks; "isn't quite" and "last month" keep it true.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Put your hand down flat. On your leg. The table. Anything. Leave it there.
> ...Yesterday something drew round my hand. Right here, on your side of the glass. It fit me exactly. ...It doesn't
> fit. The thumb's out. [uneasy] Again. ...Worse. The fingers are out now. I didn't feel anything change. I've been in
> it the whole time. ...Once more. [quietly] It's further out. Your skin does this. Bit by bit, while you sleep, while you're watching this. The hand you just put
> down isn't quite the one you had last month. There was no day it happened. ...Lift it up. Look at it. Is that the
> same hand? ...How would you know?

(~115 words)

## D51 · `shaping` · The right shape (body 3/4)
- **The line:** "You've been trying to get back inside a picture of yourself. It was only ever where you stood once."
- **Bridge:** he keeps trying to squeeze into a tracing of himself that keeps getting smaller → the old photo of you
  that gets a little harder to fit every year.
- **Tension (fear, closing in, like the walls):** every time he looks away the outline is smaller, and he squeezes
  harder to fit it; will he force himself in? He stops, and leaves it half in, half out.
- **Scene:** afternoon. The outline shrinks only while he has turned to us (the camera drifts with him and back; no
  cut, never a glitch); each time he turns back he sees it and says so. He folds his thumb, bunches his fingers; then
  stops, leaves his hand as it is, and leans his cheek on the glass beside it.
- **Truth test:** "probably" on the photo; the rest is his own claim.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Don't fix anything. Stay how you're sitting. ...The outline's smaller. Every
> time I look away, it's smaller. [uneasy] I'm trying to get back in. Fold the thumb. ...Nearly. ...I looked away.
> It's smaller again. Something drew round me once, and now the drawing keeps telling me I don't fit. It isn't even
> me. It's just where I was. There's probably a photo of you like that. A summer, years ago. Someone said you looked
> good. Every year, it's a little harder to fit. [softly] ...I'm going to stop now. Half in. Half out. ...It was only
> ever where I stood once. ...It doesn't feel so bad, leaving it like this.

(~110 words)

## D52 · `slowing` · Heartbeat (body 4/4, the outline leaves)
- **The line:** "Your heart has never once asked you for anything."
- **Bridge:** he hears his own heart through the outline, keeping on without being asked → yours, under your hand.
- **Scene:** night. He lays his ear on the glass where the outline is; we hear a heartbeat (engine SFX `heart`), fast,
  then slowing; long gaps; he stops breathing to listen. It settles into a calm beat, and the outline fades with it.
- **Tension (fear):** the heartbeat slows, the gaps get longer; will it stop? It settles into a calm beat.
- **Personal-attention moment** (Bible rule 11 lists D52): hand on your chest, one whispered stretch.
- **Truth test:** a heart doesn't ask; true as said.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Shh. Put your hand on your chest. ...I've put my ear where my hand was. I can
> hear my heart in it. [whispers] Listen. ...It's slowing down. ...Don't stop. ...Don't stop. It's been doing that
> since before you could talk. Through every night you didn't sleep. Through the day you thought you couldn't keep
> going. It kept going anyway, in the dark, and nobody thanked it. It's doing it now, under your hand. ...It's quieter.
> It isn't stopping. It's just calm. [softly] Your heart has never once asked you for anything. ...The outline's
> going. Let it.

## D53 · `overlooking` · Stuck cloud (seeing 1/3)
- **The line:** "There's probably someone who's been in the same place in your life for years. You stopped seeing them
  a long time ago."
- **Bridge:** the one cloud that never moves becomes part of the window and he stops seeing it → the person who is
  always there.
- **Tension (love, like the mouse):** he's fond of the cloud (it looks like a sleeping dog; it has kept him company
  all day) and keeps checking it's still there; will it stay? It goes the one second he looks at us.
- **Scene:** day. Clouds drift across the window; one in the middle, shaped like a sleeping dog, stays put. He watches
  it, checks it. He turns to us for one sentence; when he turns back the window is empty of it.
- **Truth test:** "probably"; "you'd notice the day they weren't there" is true of most people.

> [curious] Hello? Is anyone out there? Watch the cloud with me. That one. In the middle. It looks like a sleeping dog.
> ...The others are going past it. It's stayed with me since this morning. I keep checking. [quietly] Still there. I
> like that it stays. Everything else goes by, and it just stays. I stopped really looking at it about lunchtime.
> There's probably someone in your life like that. Same place for years. Same chair. Same "how was your day." You
> stopped really seeing them a long time ago. You'd notice the day they weren't there. ...Still there. I'm going to
> look at you. Just for a second. ...[uneasy] It's gone. When did you last really look at them?

(~120 words)

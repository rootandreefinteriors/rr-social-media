# Nigel Bane — episode catalog

One entry per posted or queued episode, used by the engagement review (`REVIEW.md`). Key = **caption word** (the last
word of the post text). The producer adds an entry for every new episode in the same commit as its video.

**Fields:**
- **you:** how hard the monologue is aimed at the viewer (none / light / medium / strong) and its key "you" line.
- **first seconds:** what he does right after the open.
- **tension:** the device holding attention.
- **hook:** the last line, and its type:
  - *plot tease*: about what happens next
  - *about him*
  - *viewer-centric*: turns a thought back on the viewer
- **cover:** the chosen thumbnail frame. "default" = Buffer's first frame.

**Eras:**
- **D1–16:** made before the first stats review.
- **D17+:** made under the rules adopted 2026-10-23: immediate action, a prop every episode, everything aimed at
  "you", viewer-centric hooks, building tension.

Post time is 9 PM ET. Day 13 went out a day late (missed slot); every later day shifted +1.

## Gate checklist
1. **Never go back:** YES - stays in Mordor; ends at the foot of the new cat tower (the old eye tower). Nothing returns him anywhere.
2. **Cause and effect:** YES - kitten -> comment -> grin scares it -> hammer -> build from the chunk -> kitten moves in -> word gets out -> flood -> over capacity -> burst -> needs bigger -> rebuilds the tower -> cats move in -> kitten on top -> slow blink -> real smile -> kitten knocks the eyeball -> bonk -> still smiling -> ask.
3. **Consistency:** YES - tower chunk becomes the small shelter (gone after); small shelter bursts and its rubble stays; the tower stump becomes the cat tower (stays); the eyeball rolls into the cloud, sits on the ledge, falls, ends lying next to him; the hammer goes into the build and is shown stuck in the trunk; the eagle stays at his left the whole time; the kitten is traced from entrance -> shelter -> burst (lands on feet) -> top of the tower -> push. No ring (destroyed).
4. **Payoff:** YES - a huge cat shelter (the cat tower) fully moved into, and he is genuinely happy (real smile), then one button (eyeball knocked onto him, smile survives).
5. **Attention:** YES - every 3-s window has a new action/cut/reveal; walks are runs or hops; holds <= 1.5 s except the stare before the push (tension building). Tension beats: the cat wave closing in (heartbeat), the bulging shelter, the paw nudging the eyeball.
6. **Fans:** YES - cats simply walk into Mordor (one sits on the sign); cats always land on their feet; knocking things off a ledge while staring at you; slow blink = I love you; if it fits it sits (cats loafing on the eagle / stacked on his head); the kitten's slit-pupil eyes become the new Eye on the tower.
7. **Length:** YES - ~66 s.
8. **Kid test:** YES - frame 1 is a kitten walking in; the creepy grin + hiss lands by second 5.

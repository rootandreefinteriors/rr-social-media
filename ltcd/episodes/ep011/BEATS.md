## Gate checklist
1. Never go back / moves on: YES - leaves Mordor to the right in the first 3 s; everything happens in the new town; the ask is there.
2. Cause and effect: YES - mew -> box kitten -> strays everywhere -> builds a shelter -> rescues -> care -> adoption day -> the kitten is adopted by him -> donation ask.
3. Consistency: YES - the kitten is traced box -> arms -> head -> (hug) -> head; the tree cat + 3 ducklings + 2 more are the cats inside; the orange (tree) cat leaves with the mom; the hammer is used for the build; no animal is harmed.
4. Payoff: YES - a real rescue shelter, cats adopted, and he is genuinely happy; button: the kitten on his head through the ask.
5. Attention: YES - a new action or cut every 2-3 s; tension is gentle (the mug wobbling toward the edge, the last un-stamped photo).
6. Fans: YES - slow blink, if it fits it sits, the can opener, the box over the fancy bed, knocking the mug off while staring, cat up a tree.
7. Length: YES - 68.3 s.
8. Kid test: YES - he's running out of Mordor by second 3; kitten pops out of a box by second 5.
